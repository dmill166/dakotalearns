## What you'll find here

- **[Courses](courses/index.md)**: full, structured courses you can learn from for free. First up: **[Computer Science 1 (Python)](courses/computer-science-1/index.md)**.
- **[Guides](guides/index.md)**: standalone walk-throughs for common starting points (installing Python, using the terminal, and more).
- **[Blog](blog/index.md)**: the learning-in-public journal, covering what I'm making, what I'm figuring out, and the occasional detour into whatever I'm curious about.

> **Today it's computer science.** That's where the courses are and where I'm headed as a teacher. But Dakota Learns isn't locked to one topic; if I'm learning it, it might show up here.

## Why free

I leaned on free and open resources alongside my degrees, all the way through school and into my career. I want to keep doing that while helping others. And here's the bigger reason: technology and computing aren't the future anymore. They're the present, and they'll keep being the present. If you have even a vague interest in learning this (for college, for a career, or just for the fun of it), I want you to be able to succeed, and I'd love to be one of the reasons you do. Everything here is free to read and learn from. If it helps you and you'd like to support more of it, you can [sponsor the work](https://github.com/sponsors/dmill166), but you'll never hit a wall.
